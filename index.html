About

My background is in economics, with a focus on how capital markets and property fundamentals interact at the deal level. I spend most of my time in underwriting models and market data — testing how a deal's return profile changes as assumptions move, and where a pro forma quietly relies on something that isn't guaranteed.

This site is where that work lives in public: deal write-ups, valuation frameworks, and the models behind them, published as I build them rather than after the fact.

A model is only as good as the assumption you're least willing to question — so that's always where I start.
