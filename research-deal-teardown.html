The deal

Two sentences orienting the reader: the asset, the market, the price, the buyer and seller, and a link to the original source. Enough that someone who never saw the news can follow what comes next.

What stood out

The one thing about the capital structure that caught my attention, stated up front. For example: the loan type, the leverage, the term, or the gap between the price paid and what the in-place income supports.

Why it was likely structured this way

My read on what the sponsor was trying to accomplish, built from public records and clearly labeled as inference where the numbers are not reported. Reported figures and my estimates are always kept separate.
